## 🛡️ Core Safety Principles (99%+ Safety Rating)
* **Zero Cabin Wiring:** Absolutely no high-voltage or secondary wires inside the passenger cabin to eliminate fire risks.
* **Rat-Proofing:** All cables enclosed in Stainless Steel (SS) braided mesh and capsaicin-infused tape.
* **Impact Shielding:** Suspension dynamos protected with 3mm Angled Aluminium Skid Plates and rubber bushing mounts.
* **Electrical Safety:** In-line automotive fuses, Schottky reverse-blocking diodes, and IP65 weatherproof bonnet junction box.

---

## 📊 Energy Harvesting Architecture (5 Sources)
1. **Roof Flexible Solar (20W):** Daytime primary solar intake.
2. **Windshield IR / Seebeck:** Heat blocking + thermal-to-electric conversion.
3. **Dashboard Panel (10W):** In-cabin light recovery.
4. **Suspension Motion Dynamos:** Kinetic power recovery from vehicle vibrations.
5. **Headlight Top Thermal Recovery:** Night-time waste heat harvesting from headlight housing.
